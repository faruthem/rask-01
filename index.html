System Specifications

01

Quadrupedal Locomotive Base

Four heavy-duty hydraulic articulated legs. Overcomes urban debris, concrete steps, and collapsed ruins where traditional wheeled or tracked units stall.

02

Spherical 360° Visor

Multi-sensor dome equipped with FLIR thermal imaging, acoustic gunshot localization, and dual LiDAR scanners for real-time 3D spatial reconstruction.

03

Multi-Payload Tactical Arm

Integrated less-lethal cycling array: high-voltage stun baton, pressurized aerosol gas discharge, and an authorized kinetic shotgun module.

04

Sealed Rescue Capsule

Rear-mounted pressurized cockpit with independent medical oxygen supply designed to extract injured civilians from active fire and chemical zones.

DEFENSIVE MATRIX

Modular Riot Shield System

Constructed from alternating titanium carbide layers and ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene. Engineered to withstand direct fire from high-caliber tactical ammunition.

  • Strobe Dazzler Array: Multi-frequency high-intensity LEDs that safely disorient aggressive targets.
  • Shock Absorption Chassis: Concussive blast dissipation system protects internal micro-circuits.
  • Infantry Escort Flank: Provides a 2-meter wide armored envelope for trailing human security personnel.
